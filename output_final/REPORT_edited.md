## How the book was made

- **The hosts' words only.** The words of callers, guests and people in played clips are not in the book. Each passage comes from one host (The Muslim Lantern or Farid Responds) in one video. A small line in capitals marks whose words follow whenever the speaker changes within a section.
- **Deletion only.** From each passage, the following were removed:
  - fillers, false starts and stammered repetitions;
  - remarks addressed to callers;
  - greetings and stream logistics.

  Punctuation, capital letters and paragraph breaks were added. No word was added, changed or reordered, and nothing was summarized or written by me. The machine check is that the words of every paragraph form a sub-sequence of the host's transcribed words, in order.
- **Without repetitions.** Where the same point is made in several videos, the passages of each sub-chapter were compared and the point is given once, in its fullest telling. The other tellings were cut or omitted.
- **Square brackets.** The only words not taken from the captions are short corrections in square brackets that replace a word garbled by the automatic captions, for example "the [fitrah]" or "[Anas] bin Malik". The limits are at most 4 words per bracket and at most max(8 words, 6%) of a passage. There are 6,065 such corrections, and the book's "About this book" page explains them.
- **Footnotes: the video and the time of every paragraph** (your request of 2026-10-31).
  - Every paragraph ends with a footnote giving the channel, the exact title of the video, and the time span in the video from its first to its last word, for example `The Muslim Lantern, "Aqeedah (Creed) And Its Significance In Islam …", 21:19–22:20.`
  - In the PDF the time is a link that opens the YouTube video at that second.
  - Notes are numbered 1, 2, 3 … on each page.
  - A further paragraph from the same video says "Ibid., 23:11–23:27". The first note on every page always names the video again, so you never have to turn back a page to find it.
  - Times come from the `[mm:ss]` timestamps of the caption lines, by aligning the edited words to the transcript. Each time is the start of the caption line that holds the word, so it is accurate to the length of one caption line (a few seconds).
- **Reference texts.** Shaded boxes give the text of a Quran verse (quran.com, Saheeh International) or a hadith (sunnah.com) cited in the passage just above. They come only from those sites, never for Bible references, and never inside the hosts' words.
- **Articles.** The 8 articles of Farid Responds are reproduced as their original pages, unaltered and checked by sha256.

## Quality notes and limitations

- **Size.** The complete book has 3,327 pages, much more than the 800–1,200 pages I first estimated. Cleaning alone kept about 78% of the words; removing repetitions brought that to 58%, and with every argument kept the book cannot be much shorter. It can be split into volumes, for example Islam (Parts I–XII) and the other religions (Parts XIII–XVI), or reduced by a more selective edit if you want.
- **Words missing from the captions.** Where the automatic captions dropped words entirely (not garbled, just missing), they could not be added, since nothing may be added. The affected half-sentences were removed. A few details were lost this way, for example Ibn Mas'ud's "111 chapters" in FR080 and some lists of reciters in FR268. Details that only needed a garbled name corrected were restored in a final pass (E045, E047, E054; see `work/edit/restore_todo.md`).
- **Some garbled names are left as captioned** because the right name could not be established with certainty: Damans, Benue, Alabri, Awiyazid, "Julian Porter and Phillip Pitcher", "Nvich National University" / "Navich", "Survivita".
- **Names identified by the editor from context.** Bracketed names such as [Hudhayfah], [Ibn Kathir], [al-Tabari], [Ibn Hazm], [Ibn Ishaq] and [Saadia] were each checked against the quoted texts and kept.
- **Attribution** relies on the speaker labels of Stage 3, the same labels the verbatim compilation used. Doubtful cases, such as auto-captioned videos without turn markers where a caption line can mix two speakers, are listed in `attribution_doubts.md`.
- **Statements are the hosts' own.** The editing guide forbids cutting a statement because it might be factually wrong. One editor (batch E014) did so twice before that rule was added on 2026-10-24: "you will be called Jacob" (ML359) and "they have 30 Gods" (ML275). A check of every figure removed from the book (`work/scripts/dropped_stats.py`) also found 28 statistics said by the hosts that were cut without a recorded reason and are no longer anywhere in the book. All of these are listed, with video and minute, in `removed_statistics.md`. Words a host quotes from someone else keep their "he said". If keeping that was not possible, the quotation was deleted, so it never reads as the host's own claim.
