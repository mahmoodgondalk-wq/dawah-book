### ML285:291-323
Here, he said that "the Sahabi have said it". 'Umar ibn al-Khattabؓ, he said that it's a good Bid'aah. So, you agree that in this case, you have a statement.

Now, praying Taraweeh in Jam'aa is a Bid'aah? There is a Hadith of the Prophetﷺ where he prayed Taraweeh, and he gathered the people and he led them in Jam'aa, and he didn't do it for one night; he did it for multiple nights. And the Hadith mentions the Prophetﷺ doing it, and the Hadith also mentions the Prophetﷺ left it. For what reason? He left it because he said: I am afraid it's going to become an obligation on you. If I continue to do it, it will become obligatory, and everyone will have to pray Taraweeh. It's not going to be a Sunnah; it's going to be an obligatory action. So the Prophet left it for that reason. When the Prophetﷺ died and the time of 'Umar ibn alkhattab came, he said, "OK, I'm going to gather the people again now."

So, this is what 'Umar ibn Alkhattab did: he brought an action that the Prophetﷺ already did, and he led the people in Jam'aa, because now it cannot become an obligation, because the Prophetﷺ died. No Shar' (Law), no revelation is going to come and say now it's an obligation.
