### FR143:51-126
This claim of "hey, there are so many religions, it's pointless to look into because there's just too many": I don't feel like a claim like that is a sincere one. And I've heard people say this. They'd say: oh, your prophet does miracles? Well, there are a thousand religions out there with prophets doing miracles. If you sincerely believed in miracles, I think you'd be worried, because you'd have a lot of people making claims about the reality of the world while being able to do all these supernatural things. I'd be curious. I'd leave my home, and I'd start looking for these people, and trying to get close to them, and trying to figure out what the heck's going on.

If there was someone that was murdered (this is my favorite example for this): Sherlock Holmes comes along, there's someone murdered, let's find out who did it. And they're bringing forth evidences, and they're looking, and they're narrowing it down, and then someone comes along: there's no point! What do you mean there's no point? Why would there be no point? There's only seven billion people in the world, it could be any one of them. I guess technically it could be, but what do you expect, every time there's a murder, for us to go through absolutely every person and write them off completely? Or are we going to work a bit more productively and try to see if there's any evidence?

If this person's been murdered in Berlin or in Ireland, I don't need to consider anyone who's never been there. I don't need to think about anyone who's in India and they've never left there. I don't need to think about people in China. It's just who was here at the scene of the crime. Oh, we found Irfan's beard hair on the floor, maybe he's a suspect now. You're looking around, you're finding evidences which suggests for you to look in a particular direction. And the same thing can be done here with anything: you go for a process of elimination, you write off categories of things that you don't even really need to consider, and then you look for things which give you reason to take it seriously, [as] a potential suspect in the case of the murderer, or as a potential guidance when it comes to things like religion.
