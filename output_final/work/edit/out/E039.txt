### FR250:1-43
This is a different way of looking at proofs for prophethood. Usually it's done by focusing on matters like miracles, matters like prophecies, matters like what you find in previous scriptures, the eloquence of the Quran, many different methods when one looks into the proofs of the prophet. However, in this particular subject I wanted to focus on the question of why did the Sahabah themselves believe.

Do you know how Aisha came to Islam? She was born into Islam. However, does that really change anything? She knew him arguably better than anyone else. She outlived the prophet, peace be upon him, by almost 50 years, and in that time she was seen as arguably the greatest scholar that we have in Islam. She taught more than most. She has more hadiths than most. She made a huge change.

Some of these topics are going to seem like: why are you even going into this? This lecture is focusing on not just the Muslims but those who are extremely skeptical. So the first subtopic will be: did she claim to believe in Islam? The one after that will be: was she sincere? Was she truthful? And then we're going to get into the topic of what are the proofs that not necessarily made her a Muslim but kept her a Muslim: why was she a sincere believer in Islam?

This is the simplest point really. Every single hadith that you read that [Aisha] is narrating — and these reports come through several companions, a large number — you can find them in pretty much every book of hadith: [Bukhari], Muslim, the six books, any book that you can think of, you're gonna find narrations of Aisha. ### FR250:63-348
