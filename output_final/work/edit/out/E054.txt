The Quran mentions six days (alA'raf7:54), which can also refer to six periods of time. So Allah is talking about creating the universe; it can be six days, and it can be six periods of time. He's telling us that he created the universe in that period of time. And then Allah also gives descriptions, for example, the heaven was one, and Allah separated it into seven (alBaqara2:29).

The word "day" in the Arabic language, the word يوم, can refer to a period of time, not only the 24 hours day that we use, but a period of time. For example, Allah in the Quran (alHajj22:47), he mentions a day that is a thousand years. Allah mentions in the Quran the day of judgement, which is 50,000 years (alMa'arij70:4). Also, there's narrations of the Prophetﷺ where he says "a day like a year, a day like a month", etc. But either or, it doesn't really matter. We could look at it whichever different way; it doesn't contradict the text.

Also things like mountains, rivers, and stuff like that: Allah has some descriptions where He mentions, for example in Surat Fussilat, a few verses in the 1st page, He mentions placing the mountains, the rivers, stuff like this (Fussilat41:10). So there are descriptions in the Quran. So through reading the Quran, I know that God is telling me how He created the universe. So my perspective on that is the perspective that Allah tells me about it.

### ML330:71-104
إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَرِثُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَنۡ عَلَیۡهَا وَإِلَیۡنَا یُرۡجَعُونَ. Paradise is not on Earth, so I don't see where the conflict is. Paradise is a different place that we believe is above, so it's a completely different location. Allah is saying that everything will return to Him. And if you finish the verse, it says وَإِلَیۡنَا یُرۡجَعُونَ, that you will return to Us. So the same verse talks about the idea of returning. Inheritance here means that everything will return to Allah, the One that created everything. This is Allah speaking. That's why it says "We". It doesn't say "I am".
