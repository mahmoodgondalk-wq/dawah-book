When it comes to going and reciting the Quran, there's two issues there. The first issue is going in congregation on a grave and reciting: I'll bring 100 people, and let's go to this grave, in congregation, we recite together or whatever it is, for the dead person, with the intention of us benefiting the dead person. That is not found in the Quran or Hadith; therefore it's something that people innovated. Now, on the other hand, reciting the Quran in a way in which it's not innovated, meaning you don't bring people and recite the Quran in the grave: let's say I'm going to recite the Quran in a way which is not innovated. I'm just normally going to read the Quran, and after reading the Quran, I have the intention that I want Allah to give the reward for this recitation to the dead person.

The first issue is the way you're going to do the recitation. If that way is not presented in Islamic texts, like congregation, going together and reciting this and that on the grave, next to the grave, then this way is innovated. Reciting the Quran itself in general, by yourself or whichever way you're going to recite the Quran, then having the intention: ”Oh Allah, give this reward to the dead person”: now, this is an issue of disagreement. This is not haram. We can't say this is necessarily haram. Some scholars will say that the reward will reach the dead person, and some people will say that the reward does not reach the dead person. There's two opinions. Some people will tell you the reward does not reach the dead person, therefore there's no point of doing that: you are just going to get the reward of recitation you did.
