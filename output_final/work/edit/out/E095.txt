### ML246:32-69
The difference between Islam and any other faith –and I think that is very important for people to understand– in Islam, the job of us, or the job of the Messengers of God, or us trying to fulfil the same Message of the Messengers of God, is not to convince anyone. This is not the attitude that any Muslim takes towards Islam. The attitude that we take towards Islam is quite simple: it is delivering the Message. Allah says in the Quran (Al'ankabut29:18) وَمَا عَلَى ٱلرَّسُولِ إِلَّا ٱلْبَلَـٰغُ ٱلْمُبِينُ (It's only upon the Messenger to give a clear demonstration and delivery of the Message).

So it's upon me as someone who is acting as a Messenger in this case –not a Messenger of God like the Prophets, no!– I'm just delivering the Message of the Messenger of God. So I'm just conveying what he said, because he said: (Convey upon me my Message to the people). So, we are conveying the Message of the Messenger of God, the Messenger of Allah.

We say to the people that Allah has sent Prophets and Messengers. These Prophets and Messengers came at different times and different localities. They had one Message, which is that there is only One Entity worthy of worship. That Entity is not like us as humans; does not have children, does not have fathers, does not have grandfathers; created the universe and gives life and takes away life from people, causes death. And it created everything around us and placed us in this life as a test, that whatever we do here in this life is a test for us. And if we choose to do good –because the morality source is from that Creator, from that God, that He reveals it to us– and if we choose to do the good that He commanded us to do, it will be good for us in this life, and it will be good for us in the Afterlife. And if we choose to disobey, and to harm people, and we'll do evil, there will be consequences in the afterlife for these actions.

So, this is essentially the Message of Islam. And these Prophets and Messengers bring revelation, and within that revelation there's the Guidance and teachings, telling you what will happen in the future, and what will happen to you when you die, etc. And we deliver that Message to the people. And this is our job as Muslims.

But trying to convince people, I believe, is already an incorrect attitude to take to anything. People have to have a genuine interest in whatever it is that they're approaching. They have to have genuine interest, they have to be sincere, and they have to look and research. And then I believe anyone who's sincere, who's open-minded, when he opens the Quran and reads, he will recognize that what he's reading there are not human words, that's not a human being writing this information, because this is a very distinguished difference –especially for people who read– between how humans write and what they say, and how the Creator addresses and speaks to His creation.
