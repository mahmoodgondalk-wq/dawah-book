### ML327:1-236
There is this issue that has been happening recently. If you use social media, if you use Twitter, if you use YouTube or any social media platform, I'm sure you're aware of the recent issue and infighting and disagreements between certain Du’aat (preachers) today in the western scene, and then you get people using certain labels like “Daawah scene is a mess” in the west.

The first thing we need to understand is that not everyone doing daawah in the west should be put in one basket. There's a lot of people, alhamdulillah, who are doing very good work, who are focusing on teaching people their religion, focusing on doing شروح Shuruh (explanations) of certain Islamic books. An example is our Brother AbdulRahman Hassan, may Allah reward him: he's focusing on Duroos (Courses), he's focusing on giving Shuruh and explanations and calling people to Tawheed (Monotheism) and teaching them about the religion. And then you got other Du'aat, MashaAllah, who are also just focused on their mission of calling people to Islam, engaging in the Daawah, and they're not engaging in infighting and disagreement between one another. I don't think it's the whole Dawah scene.

Now this issue of infighting, where is it coming from? I'd say it is initially stemming from two things. The first thing is certain people who believe this person is a person of innovation, and this individual who is preaching Innovation, preaching Falsehood and preaching wrong teachings about Islam to other people; therefore I have to warn from that specific individual, I have to warn the people: don't listen to this individual, because he's spreading false beliefs and he's misguiding individuals. And also, on top of that, the other people are saying: OK, there's those people who are pushing Liberalism, they're pushing Feminism, and they're pushing all of these things which are against our Islamic teachings, and we need to warn from those individuals. The issue is generally coming from those two things.

And warning people from that is a part of our religion without a shadow of a doubt. Prophetﷺ said: مَن رَأَى مِنكُم مُنْكَرًا فَلْيُغَيِّرْهُ بيَدِهِ، فإنْ لَمْ يَسْتَطِعْ فَبِلِسانِهِ، فإنْ لَمْ يَسْتَطِعْ فَبِقَلْبِهِ — Who sees of you an evil deed, let him change it with his hand; if he cannot, then with his tongue; if he cannot, then to hate it with his heart. So this idea of warning from Innovation or warning from people of Innovation is a part of our Islamic teachings, or warning from people who are pushing forwards false doctrines like Liberalism and Feminism, which actually attacks a person's Tawheed (Monotheism) as well, if you look deeply into this ideology.

The issue is not that certain people should be warned; the issue is that the things have been happening with zero wisdom. Things have not become warning people from Innovation, warning people from problems for the sake of Islam; they become personal issues. The problem is not the idea of warning against people with Innovation, because the Shaytaan (Satan), when he wants to cause division between the people, he has to tell you you're doing good. So I'm saying that the people who are doing these things, they're using the Hadith of the Prophetﷺ; in their mind, I know deep down, they believe they're doing something which is good. And Allahﷻ says in The Quran17:53 وَقُل لِّعِبَادِى يَقُولُوا۟ ٱلَّتِى هِىَ أَحْسَن ۚ (and say to My Servants to say that which is best) إِنَّ ٱلشَّيْطَـٰنَ يَنزَغُ بَيْنَهُمْ (Verily The Satan he is causing enmity between each other). So the way we speak has to be very balanced and has to be with wisdom.

What is the problem now? There's two main problems. The first thing is that the idea has become a personal thing. If you watch the videos of certain individuals responding to other people: "He said that about me" and "he accused me of this" and "I accused him of this." "Him" and "me"! This has not become something about Islam, or warning people from Innovation, warning people from issues towards Islam and liberalism. I want you to go open the video and listen to yourself speaking, and then think two times: has it become a personal issue between you and someone else? Or are you doing this actually for the sake of Allahﷻ?

The second problem with this idea is that some people think they are above criticism. If an individual corrects them, they get very upset, they get very angry, and instead of responding to that specific individual in an academic way, responding to the points that he made, no, they would actually rather attack him and call him a traitor and call him all sorts of names. So some people think they are above correction from certain people because of the personal issues: I have a personal issue with you, then you personally cannot correct me; everyone else on the planet, they can correct me, but you cannot follow the command of the prophet and correct me, because you are X, Y, and Z.

If you are a part of this problem, think very deeply: has this become a personal thing between you and someone else, when you keep mentioning a specific individual, keep mentioning his name day and night, and he mentions your name day and night? You say he did this to me and I did that to him; it's like little children in the playground: "Daddy, he hit me!"

I don't want this to be misunderstood, that we shouldn't warn from certain people of innovation or certain acts of innovation. We have to always weigh the harms and the benefits. I have to have wisdom and see what is the harms and the benefits behind warning from that specific individual, or perhaps I should just warn from the evil that that person is doing: I just speak about the evil that the person has done instead of warning from that specific individual. And the Prophetﷺ, for example, he said: أقيلوا ذَوي الهيئاتِ عثَراتِهم. He said: the people of good manners or doing good, then avoid criticizing of the little things that they do; don't look at their slips and then attack them on their slips. Have some mercy towards your brother.

I'm not saying you cannot speak publicly, but I was talking about wisdom: where is the benefit for the Muslims? It's not about personal things and doing it publicly; it's about: is it more beneficial to speak to him privately, or is it more beneficial not to speak to the individual privately, weighing the harms and the benefits, looking at the individual. Perhaps you misunderstood what that person said, perhaps you don't have the full image.

I'm involved in the Daawah scene in the west, and there are many Duaat that I advise privately, and I give them advice and I say to them, and they give me advice and they say to me certain things: "OK brother, you said the specific thing; this specific thing is not correct." We have to accept advice; people have to be given advice to one another. This is not the issue; the issue is how we're behaving towards that criticism that is coming towards us.

Now we understood where the problem is coming from. Now I want to talk about the harm effects: what are the harm effects of this infighting, of this behavior that we see on social media, of this person name calling another person? Allahﷻ says in the Quran49:11 وَلَا تَنَابَزُوا بِالْأَلْقَابِ ۖ (Do not throw titles at one another). Then Allahﷻ says something very important, He says: بِئْسَ الِاسْمُ الْفُسُوقُ بَعْدَ الْإِيمَانِ — Verily it is an evil title to have, to be called the Fasiq (a disobedient) after you were a Mu'min (Believer), just by this idea of name calling each other.

So what are the harm effects of this childish behavior that we see? The first thing is the image of Islam! The image of Islam publicly, the image of Islam to people who are not Muslim. The haters of Islam, the Islamophobs, they make videos and reactions, and then they use this as content to show: look how Muslims are fighting with one another! So we have the islamophobes using this content for their benefit, and then we have the people who are just laymen watching: "Look at these people. I won't be a part of this, just infighting, people bickering with another. Why would I want to be a part of people who are not united with each other?"

The harm effects also include the people confusing the masses. The Muslims are confused: this person is speaking about this, this person's speaking about that, he's bringing some Ahadith, he's bringing some Ahadith... Who has the truth? People are confused. They do not know what's going on. They do not know حق from باطل. That's their fault, I would say, initially, for not seeking knowledge, but it is a reality that you're confusing the people. Not everyone is a knowledgeable person. People watching you, listening to what you say, it's not clear for them as you think: "Oh, I've said it and I put some of these references, therefore now everyone knows I'm upon the truth!" It's not how it works.

One of the major problems as well is the distrust towards the Duaat or the Religious People, Religious figures. People now are thinking: OK, this person is an innovator, this person is bad, this person is bad, therefore what I'm gonna do now, I'm not going to listen to anyone; I don't know who to listen to, I'm just going to listen to myself. And I've seen the harm effects that those Duaat are doing in the west. I get reverts saying: "All those people are warning from each other; I just pick and choose, I follow the Quran, my own understanding, and I take from different people who I think..." Because the person is confused now: he sees those people on the scene, they're all disagreeing with each other, he doesn't know who should he follow and who shouldn't he follow.

And also the harm effects of the infighting between the Muslims, Muslims fighting each other: they've got now cheerleaders for every group. Those cheerleaders are going getting videos from this person, and the other cheerleaders are going getting videos from the other person. They're creating channels, and in these channels they post the clips about the other individual: "Oh, he made this mistake", "oh, he made that mistake". After all of this childish behavior, you're trying to come to us and convince us that what you're doing right now, this childish behavior, is for the sake of Allah ﷻ! If it was for the sake of Allah ﷻ you wouldn't be doing it this way. Allah ﷻ knows the intentions. I know that some people think they're sincere, but look at your behavior, come back, see: did Shaytan deceive you in what you're doing or not?

So what is the solution now? How do we fix this issue that we have? Because these issues need to be discussed, they need to be talked about. I'm going to give two advice: one advice is for the people who are doing Daawah and one advice for people who are watching the content. Starting with the Duaat -or I don't even know if they're worthy of using that term Duaat- people who are in the west who are trying to do something for the sake of Allah ﷻ.

First thing is humbling ourselves, taking the criticism from other individuals. There was an example for one Brother doing Daawah -I'm not going to mention his name- he was advised by another Brother. Alhamdulillah, he accepted the advice. He made a video, academic video, responding to the points that the other brother has made. Both of them were using أخلاق, using good manners, Alhamdulillah, behaving well and humbling themselves, accepting criticism, listening to the other point of view. It was done publicly! The person, he did the video about him publicly, and he responded to him publicly with respect, as someone who's got أخلاق, as a Muslim should be behaving. So that's the first thing that we need to do. We humble ourselves, we accept criticism from other individuals, and we understand that this criticism should be for the sake of Allah ﷻ.

The second idea is: if you want to warn, try to look at the harms and the benefits and whether it's better to warn against the issue or the individual. Avoid making these refutation personal, mentioning his name, calling him names. Avoid doing that, because you yourself know very well that's not the teachings of Islam.

Now if you want to warn against an individual, there's another thing, a solution. If you want to warn against an individual, you warn against him once or twice, or three times. You want to warn against a person who you think he's an innovator, warn against the innovator. Two, three times you warned against the Innovator. Alhamdulillah, Khalass, everyone who follows you now knows you believe X person is an innovator. But to be making videos about that individual, you keep making videos every couple of months, you keep coming back to the same idea, and you're saying: "Oh, it's just I want the people more to know that this person is an innovator." You think the people who follow you don't know? I'm sure they've watched 100 videos that you've already done. So if it's not a personal issue, you wouldn't be making this number of videos on a specific individual.

Now, acting with manners and good أخلاق: Wallahi, this is the most important thing. The person is more likely to accept your advice. If you are doing this for the sake of Allah, and you want that Allah ﷻ accept this as a Good Deed from you, and you're doing it because you're worried about the Deen, then have good أخلاق. Stop naming, calling each other; stop behaving like children. If you're behaving like a child, this is the Deen of Allahﷻ. Your videos, what you're making on social media, has an effect on many people. Hundreds of thousands of people. It's not a game. You have to act in good أخلاق, in good manners. It's not a game. "I got you!" and then you scream and you go to that person, you go to this person, you try to get cameras and make noise. This, is it about you? Or is it about the Deen of Allahﷻ?

This would be my advice of how to solve this issue. If we humble ourselves, we act with أخلاق towards each other, someone gives Naseeha (or advice), tries to see the harms and the benefits. If it's done publicly, then other person has been criticized publicly with good أخلاق, with evidences. Then he also responds with good أخلاق, with evidences. If he's wrong, he admits he's wrong. He's not wrong, then he responds showing his evidences from the Quran and Sunnah. We don't have an issue with this idea of giving Naseeha to each other publicly; the issue is the responses and the behavior that we're using. This is the issue.

And the other solution I want to mention, it is for the people who are watching these videos. You are a huge part of this problem. First, the infighting between Muslims: the cheerleaders who go under everyone's post cursing this person, and go to the other person, they curse this person and they start calling him names. All of you will be accountable in the Day of Judgment. Do not think that the people who you're following are the ones who will be accountable on your behalf. You will be accountable for what you do. And Allahﷻ says in the Quran99:8 وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍۢ شَرًّۭا يَرَهُۥ — the weight of an atom! Your whole post that you type on social media is more than an atom, and you will see it, as Allahﷻ says it in سورة الزلزلة (Azzalzala Chapter). You will see it on the Day of Judgment in front of you. These lies and those cursing and those name calling, everything you're doing on social media, on the day of judgment it will be in your Saheefa (Deeds' Accounting Book), and I would prefer to have my Saheefa empty from any harming other Muslims in the day of judgment. May Allahﷻ guide us. So the people, they need to stop this idea of being cheerleaders, this person against this person.

And the second thing is: boycott those people. If a person keeps causing infighting and issues, just boycott them! Don't watch their content. Go watch something that is beneficial for you, especially when it is people who are responding to each other on personal issues. Go learn something beneficial. Listen to teachings about the Quran and the Sunnah, Books explaining Hadith, Books explaining what you need to learn, what is going to benefit you in your Akhira (Afterlife), when you die. What is going to benefit you in your grave? It's your Deeds! It's not going and liking these videos that every person is going to make towards the other person, and just looking for drama and looking for fighting. And if you love this fighting between the brothers, you have a disease in your heart.

So we need to humble ourselves, all of us, starting by me, first. And we need to learn to have better أخلاق; may Allahﷻ give me and give everyone better أخلاق. If you're a part of this problem, try, for the sake of Allahﷻ, make Du'aa to Allahﷻ. Listen to the points that I mentioned and make Du'aa to Allahﷻ: is this a personal issue? Should I be continuing this childish behavior that I'm doing? Does this benefit here, outweighing the harm of what I'm doing? Is it really for the sake of Allahﷻ? Or should I now move on and focus on either the problem, talking about the problem, or doing Daawah, or teaching people Tawheed (Monotheism), or doing something beneficial? May Allahﷻ guide us all to The Truth.
